The problem

Homes waste energy in empty rooms.

Lights, air conditioning, TVs and chargers keep running in rooms nobody is using. Fixed schedules barely help, because they switch things off by the clock rather than by who is actually home.

The solution

An AI-powered web platform that bridges IoT with predictive intelligence.

PresenceIQ learns your household's unique occupancy patterns over time, automatically optimizing lighting, climate and appliances so energy is used only where and when it's needed.

Key features

Everything a home needs to stop wasting energy

Smart Automation

Powers down devices and adjusts climate in vacant rooms, and switches the whole home to eco-mode when you leave.

Occupancy Simulation

Boosts security while you're away by replaying your natural routine across lights and devices.

Energy Cost Optimizer

Uses live utility rates to shift high-consumption appliance tasks to cheaper, off-peak hours.

Analytics Dashboard

Tracks real-time consumption and cost savings, with practical recommendations to cut waste further.

Measurable Savings

Lowers utility costs by eliminating passive energy drain.

Zero-Effort Comfort

Pre-conditions rooms from learned habits, with no manual programming.

Methodology

Five steps, running in a continuous loop

PresenceIQ senses activity, predicts patterns and automates energy controls, then measures the result and learns from it.

  1. Step 1

    Sense

    Data collection

    Motion sensors, door contacts and smart meters track room occupancy and device-level power draw in real time.

  2. Step 2

    Learn

    Predictive AI

    Machine learning analyzes usage history to find daily routines and predict upcoming room occupancy.

  3. Step 3

    Optimize

    Cost & load scheduling

    Predicted occupancy is matched with live utility pricing to schedule climate changes and heavy tasks off-peak.

  4. Step 4

    Act

    Automated execution

    Adjusts AC, lighting, water heating and EV charging, and runs occupancy simulation while residents are away.

  5. Step 5

    Evaluate

    Feedback loop

    Benchmarks actual use against baseline consumption, shows savings on the dashboard and refines its predictions.
